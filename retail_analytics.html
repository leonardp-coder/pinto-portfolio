01 · Pipeline & Modelling Flow

From raw retail logs to decision-ready views

Structured into four clean stages so business users can filter by period, region, and product category without slow ad-hoc queries.

01 · INGEST Raw Retail Extracts Orders, line items, product catalog, customer & calendar tables.
02 · CLEANSE Standardization & QA Null handling, date alignment, currency/discount normalization, deduplication.
03 · MODEL Star Schema & Measures Fact sales linked to conformed Date, Product, Customer, and Region dimensions.
04 · VISUALIZE Interactive Dashboard Executive KPIs, trend lines, category comparisons, and cross-filtering.
